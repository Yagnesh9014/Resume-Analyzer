How the analysis works

See what happens to your resume before a recruiter does.

A resume has to pass several checks before its strongest points get noticed. Resume Analyzer breaks that journey into understandable signals and turns them into practical feedback you can act on.

01

Upload

Your PDF resume is securely sent to the analyzer.

02

PDF Parsing

Text is extracted so the resume can be evaluated as content, not just as a document.

03

ATS Review

Keyword optimization, formatting, skills and content quality are evaluated.

04

AI Review

Your projects, education and experience are reviewed together for recruiter readiness.

05

Improve

Get specific improvements, weak areas and suitable entry-level roles.

What we analyze

  • ✓Keyword Optimization — how clearly relevant skills and terms are represented.
  • ✓ATS Formatting — structure and readability for automated screening.
  • ✓Technical Skills — the technical strengths demonstrated by the resume.
  • ✓Experience & Projects — how effectively practical work supports the profile.
  • ✓Content Quality — clarity, relevance and overall resume communication.

What you get back

  • →ATS Score with a transparent five-part breakdown.
  • →Professional Summary based on the information in your resume.
  • →Strengths & Weak Skills so you know what stands out and what needs work.
  • →Recruiter Review covering strengths, risks and entry-level readiness.
  • →Action Plan with improvement suggestions and recommended job roles.
